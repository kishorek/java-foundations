Step 1

Standard store. src/main/java (fresh food), src/test/java (spoons mirror), pom.xml (shopping list). mvn test = taste EVERYTHING.

pom.xml essentials
<!-- pom.xml essentials -->
<project>
  <groupId>com.shop</groupId>
  <artifactId>tasks</artifactId>
  <version>1.0</version>
  <properties>
    <maven.compiler.release>17</maven.compiler.release>
  </properties>
  <dependencies>
    <dependency>
      <groupId>org.junit.jupiter</groupId>
      <artifactId>junit-jupiter</artifactId>
      <version>5.10.0</version>
      <scope>test</scope>
    </dependency>
  </dependencies>
</project>
test scope = spoons never ship in jars. Pin release 17 for identical builds.
